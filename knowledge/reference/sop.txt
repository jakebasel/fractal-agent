03 — SIGNAL CONFIGURATIONS (5 Types)

       M           Manipulation — Simple M move. Market intends opposite direction. Wait for reversal setup.


       2M          Double Manipulation — M in both directions. Price retraces back to first untouched 5M fair-value gap between them.


       ■           Triangle Pattern — M in one direction + DB in the other. Conflicting signals. Use extreme caution.


       DB          Double Break — Continuation signal. Dealer reveals hand. Trade in DB direction ONLY, or sit out completely.


   2DB             Double DB (both directions) — Voids setup entirely. DO NOT ENTER. Walk away.
