            MARKET TRANSLATOR ENTRY MODEL
The Market Translator strategy is built around two simple signals: M and DB. M tells us to look
for a move in the opposite direction, while DB tells us to look for the same direction after a
retracement. Once the direction is clear, we follow the charts step-by-step — 5-minute, 1-minute,
then 30-second — waiting for each Fair Value Gap (FVG) to be created and tapped before
moving to the next timeframe. The final candle closure gives us the entry.

Here’s how it works:

STEP 2 — 5-MINUTE FVG

Once we know the direction:

Wait for the first 5-minute FVG to be created in that direction.

But…

THE 5M FVG MUST BE TAPPED.

No tap = no trade.

STEP 3 — 1-MINUTE FVG

After the 5M FVG is tapped:

Immediately drop to the 1-minute chart.

Wait for the first 1-minute FVG to be created in the expected direction.

Then:
It must be tapped.

STEP 4 — 30-SECOND FVG

Now drop to 30 seconds.

Wait for the first 30-second FVG to be created in the expected direction.

Again:

STEP 5 — THE ENTRY

After the 30-second FVG is tapped:

Wait for the candle sequence to close in the expected direction.

Then:

STOP LOSS: BEGINNING OF THE LEG THAT CREATED THE 30-SECOND FVG.
That is typically the same leg that created/tapped the 1-minute FVG and then created the 30-
second FVG.

Mental checklist:

M: M → OPPOSITE → 5M → TAP → 1M → TAP → 30S → TAP → CLOSE → ENTER

DB: DB → RETRACE → SAME → 5M → TAP → 1M → TAP → 30S → TAP → CLOSE →
ENTER

And the golden rule: If ANY step doesn't happen, there is NO entry.

Super simple explanation

   •   The Market Translator paints a Reversal Zone.
   •   This tells us the market could be getting ready to turn around.
   •   We don’t jump in right away.
   •   We wait for price to come back into the zone.
   •   Once price is there, we look for a 1-minute Fair Value Gap.
   •   From that 1-minute FVG, we wait for the first 30-second FVG.
   •   When the 30-second FVG closes over a series of candles, we have our entry.
   •   Then we trade in the direction of the reversal.

Super simple explanation

  •   Spotlight says: “BUY” or “SELL.”
  •   Don’t chase it.
  •   Wait for price to come back.
  •   Price comes into the dealing range.
  •   Look for a Fair Value Gap.
  •   When price is ready to move again, enter in the same direction as the Spotlight signal.
  •   We want the move to reach about 0.5 to 1 standard deviation.

Signal → Pullback → FVG → Entry → Move → 0.5–1 SD
